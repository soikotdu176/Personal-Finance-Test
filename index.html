Excel & Budge CSV Migration

Import worksheets from Assets & Liabilities.xlsx, Income & Expenses.xlsx, or a Budge app export (budge-*.csv / .xlsx). Header rows are detected automatically and re-imports skip duplicates. The original files are never modified.

Migration History

Each import is tracked as a batch. Deleting a batch also removes the records it imported.

Office Purchase & Bill

Import the FY-wise Office Purchase & Bill.xlsx workbook (sheets such as 2025-26, left cash outflow / right cash inflow). Rows are deduplicated and each import is tracked as a migration batch. Imported entries appear on the Office Purchase & Bill page, and any entry linked to a Cash & Bank account from Manage Categories also moves that account's balance.

Export produces the same side-by-side layout, so the file can be re-imported without duplicates.
